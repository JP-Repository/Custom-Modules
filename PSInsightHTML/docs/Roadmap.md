# Roadmap after 0.9.0

These are research or future implementation areas, not 0.9.0 capabilities:

- Month-over-month comparison and historical trends with explicit snapshot provenance.
- Large-table scale research, including virtualization or alternative data delivery while preserving an offline option.
- Forest, Domain, Domain Controller, Trust, and DFSR assessment providers.
- Additional topology visualization for relationships and site or network layout.
- Further accessibility validation with keyboard, screen reader, contrast, and print reviews across browsers.

The generic engine should remain independent of provider-specific source systems and rules.
